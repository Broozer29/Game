Patch notes:
Fire Fighter items buffed so that ignite and Ephemeral Blaze builds are expected to be more effective against bosses. Additionally, removed the cooldown reduction from Battery and introduced a new item that provides the cooldown reduction required for Electroshred builds.

Items:
- Fire without gas is just ass:
    - Ignite damage reduction: 40% -> 0%
- Eterna Burn:
    - Ignite damage reduction: 25% -> 15%
- Ephemeral Blaze:
    - Ignite damage reduction: 15% -> 0%
    - Flamethrower damage increase: 10% -> 25%
- Scorching Fury:
    - Ignite damage increase: 15% -> 20%
- Battery:
    - Cooldown reduction: 4% -> 0%
- Recycler (NEW):
    - Captain only
    - Item rarity: Rare
    - Item cost: 225$
    - Enemies damaged by your laserbeam or Electroshred gain a 10% (+10%) chance to spawn a Recycle Part on death. Picking up a Recycle Part instantly restores a charge for your secondary attack.

Soundtracks:
- Removed 1 track
- Added 2 new tracks

Bug fixes:
- Module Scorch fireballs now apply ignite as intended
- Placeholder drones now use their intended attack speed

New balance update, the patch notes:

The intent of this balance update:
The mid-game to late-game is laughably easy. The player consistently grows faster than the enemies.
In my experience snowballing is still a big problem. I am happy with the early-game balance, and
these changes should not affect it much.

Items:
- Overclock:
    - Rarity: Uncommon -> Rare
    - Price: 125 -> 225
    - Attack speed bonus: 15% -> 30%
- Guardian Drone:
    - Rarity: Uncommon -> Rare
    - Price: 125 -> 225

Enemies:
- Speed at which enemies gain strength increased
- Enemies become even quicker for every boss defeated (up to 5)
    - The intended design is that boss number 5 becomes the final boss, but it does not exist yet.

Bosses:
- Now have slightly increased movement speed for each boss defeated
- Increased maximum health growth per enemy level

God Run Detector:
- More sensitive, and now reacts a bit faster.
- New god run score added:
    1. Enemies spawn 35% more often and in larger quantities
    2. Enemies can now spawn from the LEFT side
    3. Enemy hitpoint scaling is increased. Enemies spawn in larger quantities. Enemy strength increases quicker
    4. NEW: Certain enemies can now spawn from ABOVE or BELOW

General:
- Shop Freeze (NEW):
    - A FREEZE SHOP button now sits next to Refresh
    - Freezing the shop causes the current rows of items to become locked in place and will remain for your next shop visit.
- Quitting to the main menu and pressing CONTINUE RUN while shopping now brings back the same shop:
- Settings page (NEW):
    - Open it from the pause window or from the main menu
    - CONTROLS tab: rebind button input to your own liking!
    - SOUND tab: control the volume to suit your preference!
- Pausing the game no longer simply displays text and offers you a menu with options.
- The controller input mappings has been reworked to represent your own custom mappings
- Damage numbers are no longer drawn

Items:

  - Item quality: Rare
  - Item cost: 225$
  - Effect: Your shield explodes upon breaking creating an explosion dealing 200% (+200%) damage and stuns non-boss enemies for 2 seconds.
- Barrier Booster (NEW):
  - Item quality: Common
  - Item cost: 125$
  - Effect: Increases maximum shield by 20%
- Barrier Backfire (NEW):
  - Item quality: Legendary 
  - Item cost: 300$
  - Effect: Missiles that strike your shield have a 25% chance to reflect back to their owner. Reflected missiles deal 400% of their damage.

    - Item quality: Legendary 
    - Item cost: 300$
    - EFfect:
      - Old: Deal 25% (+25%) additional damage. This bonus is lost for 3 seconds after taking damage.

- Distributed Heat (NEW) :
    - Item quality: Firefighter Relic
    - Item cost: 425$
    - Effect: Your flamethrower now fires in ALL directions but at reduced intensity. Flamethrower deals 25% reduced damage.

    - Item quality: Rare -> Legendary

- Upgrade: Focus Fire:

        - Drones now keep firing on their own as well

    - Renamed from Electro Shedding
    - Item quality: Captain legendary
    - Effect:
      - Old: Electroshred now permanently reduces enemy armor by 1 whenever it deals damage. Losing armor increases ALL damage taken.
      - New: Electroshred damage is increased by up to 100% (+100%) based on the percentage of your current shield hitpoints. Also applies to the Electroshred of your drones.

Classes:
- Firefighter:
    - Flamethrower damage and ignite apply ticks: every 0.28 seconds -> every 0.22 seconds
    - Ignite damage: about 18% higher
    - Ignite duration: 1.75 seconds -> 2.35 seconds

Enemies:
- Enemies now get stronger smoothly during a run instead of in steps after each defeated boss
    - This covers movement speed, armor, knockback, detection range and the other bonuses enemies gain
    - The bonus now goes up to 7 instead of 5
    - Spawn cost reduction at the highest bonus: 25% -> 35%, so more enemies appear
- Shuriken Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.25 -> 0.4
    - Now drops a mine every 2.5 seconds
- Mother Ship Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.15 -> 0.3
    - Maximum drones at once: 4 -> 4 plus the difficulty bonus
- Laserbeam Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.35 -> 0.5
- Defender Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.35 -> 0.4
- Mirage Mini Boss:
    - Spawns 1 additional clone

Bosses:
- Health scaling multiplier decreased from: x1.35 -> x1.2
- Red Boss:
    - Health: 4500 -> 4400
    - Four-directional drones per cast: 2 -> 3
    - Most drones alive at once: 6 -> 7
    - Drone spawn cooldown: 25 seconds -> 22 seconds plus 2 seconds per drone still alive
        - It no longer speeds up below 25% health
    - Shuriken spawn interval: 10 seconds -> 9 seconds
- Yellow Boss:
    - Health: 5000 -> 4500
    - Damage: 9 -> 7
    - Bosses you must defeat before it can appear: 1 -> 2

Controls:
- Controllers now work through a new system that recognises many more controller brands
- Controllers can be plugged in and out while the game runs
    - You see a CONTROLLER CONNECTED or DISCONNECTED notice
    - A free controller joins on any button press
    - A controller that joins mid-level plays from the next level
    - Every controller gets its own ship, up to 16 controllers
    - A controller that drops out can reconnect to its own ship
- The game pauses when nobody can play
- Controller buttons for flying:
    - Fire: A (rebindable)
    - Special attack: B (rebindable)
    - Auto fire: LB (rebindable)
    - Pause: Menu button
    - The button that requested the main controller is gone, the main controller is now the lowest numbered one
- Keyboard: **Just use the controller man, it's just better.**
    - Fire: Space, Special attack: Q, Auto fire: E (all rebindable)
    - Enter no longer fires the special attack
    - The keyboard now steers player 1 even when a controller is connected
    - Y no longer ends the level (none of you noticed anyway)
- Menus now react to the keyboard the moment a key is pressed, and to the controller stick and d-pad in every menu
    - Arrow keys, Enter, Space and Escape work in menus
    - Input held while a screen opens is ignored until you let go
    - Score card and game over now say "Press any key to continue" right away

Performance:
- The game starts noticeably faster
- Rotating images no longer causes the game to slowly but permanently degrade in performance
- The game can properly handle large quantities of objects
- Laser beams no longer cause the game to drastically slow down
- All mini bosses are now loaded at startup, they should no longer slow the game whilst spawning in

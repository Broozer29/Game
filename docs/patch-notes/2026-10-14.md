Patch notes

General:
- Shop Freeze (NEW):
    - A free FREEZE SHOP button now sits next to Refresh
    - Items you do not buy come back in the same slots of the next shop, with the same rarity and price
    - Slots you bought stay empty, and rows that unlock after you froze the shop get new items
    - Only items for sale freeze: locked rows and bought slots are left alone
    - A frozen item is replaced by a new one if it can no longer be offered, for example a one-copy item you now own
    - Refreshing the shop unfreezes it
    - The frozen shop is saved with your run
- Quitting to the main menu and pressing CONTINUE RUN while shopping now brings back the same shop:
    - Bought slots, refresh cost, free refreshes left and Bonus Kaart's double purchase are all kept
- Settings page (NEW):
    - Open it from the pause window or from the main menu
    - CONTROLS tab: rebind fire, special attack and auto fire for keyboard and controller, with a RESET button
    - SOUND tab: music and effects volume from 0% to 100% in steps of 10%
    - Leaving with unsaved changes asks if you want to save them
- Pause window:
    - Now a proper menu with RESUME, SETTINGS and EXIT RUN
    - EXIT RUN asks if you are sure, then returns to the main menu. CONTINUE RUN restarts the level you were on
    - You can now pause without waiting, and also while flying to the portal
- Main menu:
    - The controls picture and controls text are gone, rebinding now lives in the settings page
- Shop Refresh:
    - The hover text now says the cost is 15% of your minerals, which is what it always cost (it said 25%)

Items:
- Bombastic Barrier (NEW) (Generic rare):
    - Item cost: 225$
    - Your shield explodes upon breaking creating an explosion dealing 200% (+200%) damage and stuns non-boss enemies for 2 seconds.
- Barrier Booster (NEW) (Generic common):
    - Item cost: 125$
    - Increases maximum shield by 20%
- Barrier Backfire (NEW) (Generic legendary):
    - Item cost: 300$
    - Missiles that strike your shield have a 25% chance to reflect back to their owner. Reflected missiles deal 400% of their damage.
- Blizzard Barrier (Generic legendary):
    - Renamed from Calm in chaos
    - Old: Deal 25% (+25%) additional damage. This bonus is lost for 3 seconds after taking damage.
    - New: Whilst your shields are above 1% health, they generate a storm of ice dealing up to 400% (+400%) damage per second based on the current hitpoints of your shield.
- Distributed Heat (NEW) (Firefighter relic):
    - Item cost: 425$
    - Your flamethrower now fires in ALL directions but at reduced intensity. Flamethrower deals 25% reduced damage.
- Fuel Cannister (Firefighter):
    - Rarity: Rare -> Legendary
    - Price: 225 -> 300
- Upgrade: Focus Fire (Captain relic):
    - Renamed from Module: Focus Fire
    - Old: Your drones no longer fire automatically. After your laserbeam hits a target, all drones immediately fire 1 shot towards the target.
    - New: After your laserbeam hits a target, all drones immediately fire 1 shot towards the target.
        - Drones now keep firing on their own as well
    - No longer offered to the Firefighter
    - Can now be combined with Module: Accuracy and Module: Synergize
- Electro Shredding (Captain legendary):
    - Renamed from Electro Shedding
    - Old: Electroshred now permanently reduces enemy armor by 1 whenever it deals damage. Losing armor increases ALL damage taken.
    - New: Electroshred damage is increased by up to 100% (+100%) based on the percentage of your current shield hitpoints. Also applies to the Electroshred of your drones.
- Upgrade: Overload (NEW) (Captain rare):
    - Item cost: 225$
    - Drones deal an additional 15% damage.
- Upgrade: Speed (NEW) (Captain rare):
    - Item cost: 225$
    - Drones gain 15% increased attack speed.
- Upgrade: Precision (NEW) (Captain legendary):
    - Item cost: 300$
    - Drones gain 20% chance to critically strike, dealing double damage.
- Anion Inverter (Captain relic):
    - Electroshred cooldown reduction: 60% -> 70%
- Module: Electric Razor (Captain relic):
    - Drone Electroshred copy cooldown: 1.5 seconds -> 0.5 seconds
- Plasma Bullets (Captain and Carrier common):
    - Damage over time: 300% (+300%) -> 400% (+400%)
    - Duration: 1.5 seconds -> 2 seconds
- Plasma Launcher (Captain rare):
    - Plasma shot damage: 200% (+200%) -> 250% (+250%)
- Placeholder (Generic relic):
    - Maximum stationary drones: 4 -> 8
    - Drone upgrades are now twice as effective on stationary drones, for both damage and attack speed
        - Old: damage was 150% of your drone damage multiplier, and attack speed upgrades did nothing
        - New: every drone damage or attack speed bonus counts double. Without drone upgrades they deal a third less damage than before
    - The shop now shows their real damage per second, including your drone upgrades
- Recycler (Captain rare):
    - Recycle Part drop chance per kill: 100% -> 7.5% (+7.5%)
        - It used to drop a Recycle Part on every kill by accident
- Wondrous Wisdomball (Generic relic):
    - Base chance for a wondrous refresh: 20% -> 30%
    - Chance added after each refresh that did not trigger: 17.5% -> 20%
- Guillotine (Generic legendary):
    - Removed from the game
- Piercing Rounds (Generic rare):
    - Removed from the game

Classes:
- Drones:
    - Time between shots: 0.5 seconds -> 0.75 seconds
- Firefighter:
    - Flamethrower damage ticks: every 0.28 seconds -> every 0.22 seconds
    - Ignite damage: about 18% higher
    - Ignite duration: 1.75 seconds -> 2.35 seconds

Enemies:
- Enemies now get stronger smoothly during a run instead of in steps after each defeated boss
    - This covers movement speed, armor, knockback, detection range and the other bonuses enemies gain
    - The bonus now goes up to 7 instead of 5
    - Spawn cost reduction at the highest bonus: 25% -> 35%, so more enemies appear
- Shuriken Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.25 -> 0.4
    - Now drops a mine every 2.5 seconds
- Mother Ship Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.15 -> 0.3
    - Maximum drones at once: 4 -> 4 plus the difficulty bonus
- Laserbeam Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.35 -> 0.5
- Defender Mini Boss:
    - Movement speed bonus per difficulty step: 0.35 -> 0.4
- Mirage Mini Boss:
    - Always has at least 1 extra clone, even at the start of a run

Bosses:
- Health multiplier per monster level: x1.35 -> x1.2
- Red Boss:
    - Health: 4500 -> 4400
    - Four-directional drones per cast: 2 -> 3
    - Most drones alive at once: 6 -> 7
    - Drone spawn cooldown: 25 seconds -> 22 seconds plus 2 seconds per drone still alive
        - It no longer speeds up below 25% health
    - Shuriken spawn interval: 10 seconds -> 9 seconds
- Yellow Boss:
    - Health: 5000 -> 4500
    - Damage: 9 -> 7
    - Bosses you must defeat before it can appear: 1 -> 2

Controls:
- Controllers now work through a new system that recognises many more controller brands
- Controllers can be plugged in and out while the game runs
    - You see a CONTROLLER CONNECTED or DISCONNECTED notice
    - A free controller joins on any button press
    - A controller that joins mid-level plays from the next level
    - Every controller gets its own ship, up to 16 controllers
    - A controller that drops out can reconnect to its own ship
- The game pauses when nobody can play
- Controller buttons for flying:
    - Fire: A (rebindable)
    - Special attack: B (rebindable)
    - Auto fire: LB (rebindable)
    - Pause: Menu button
    - The button that requested the main controller is gone, the main controller is now the lowest numbered one
- Keyboard:
    - Fire: Space, Special attack: Q, Auto fire: E (all rebindable)
    - Enter no longer fires the special attack
    - The keyboard now steers player 1 even when a controller is connected
    - Y no longer ends the level (it was a test shortcut)
- Menus now react to the keyboard the moment a key is pressed, and to the controller stick and d-pad in every menu
    - Arrow keys, Enter, Space and Escape work in menus
    - Input held while a screen opens is ignored until you let go
    - Score card and game over now say "Press any key to continue" right away

Performance:
- The game starts noticeably faster
- Damage numbers are no longer drawn, because in busy levels too many of them piled up and caused slow motion
- Pictures are cached with a memory limit, so the game uses less memory
- Rotated pictures now use whole degrees only
- Collision checks are faster
- Laser beams load faster at startup
- All mini bosses are now loaded at startup, so the first one in a run no longer freezes the game

Bug fixes:
- Auto attack:
    - No longer stops responding for a while after starting a new run or pressing CONTINUE RUN
- Unplugging a controller:
    - No longer leaves a fire button or direction stuck
- Shop and stick:
    - The stick no longer keeps moving the cursor in the first seconds of the shop, and the relic cursor reacts at once
- Shop refresh cost:
    - Now includes the interest from Compound Wealth
- Difficulty screen:
    - The cards no longer overlap on smaller windows
- Game over screen:
    - The last picture in the list can now appear
- Song progress bar:
    - No longer starts full at the beginning of a level
- Continue run:
    - The level progress bar and enemy spawns now follow the normal pace after pressing CONTINUE RUN
- Advanced Optics:
    - Is no longer offered after 8 copies, which is 100% critical strike chance
- Wondrous Wisdomball:
    - Copying your inventory no longer also copies a Legendary on top
- Corrosive Oil:
    - Now lowers armor once per burn instead of on every burn tick
- Gasoline:
    - Its explosion burn now follows your ignite items
- Fire Shield, lingering flames and the flamethrower:
    - Their ignite now follows your ignite items too
- Adrenaline:
    - Taking damage again now refreshes the health regeneration as well as the attack speed
- Bonus Kaart:
    - Quitting and pressing CONTINUE RUN in the shop no longer gives the double purchase back
- Mini bosses:
    - Early mini bosses no longer pile up in the list every level, which changed the odds
- Royal Guard Shieldbearer:
    - The limit of 10 at a time now counts Shieldbearers instead of Barricades
- Enemy formations:
    - Formations moving left now start fully off screen instead of half on screen
- Boss crashes:
    - Striker Boss bombing runs and Twin Boss manoeuvres no longer crash the game when their boost sound is busy
    - Yellow Boss reflective blocks no longer crash on a missile without a flight path
- Level portal:
    - The portal now only appears after you picked your relic, so you can no longer skip the relic choice
- Orbiting drones and missiles:
    - Now keep even spacing around their target
- Multiplayer:
    - The game over check no longer fails when all ships die in the same moment

- Performance logging with the options game.loadStatsDir and game.cacheStatsDir

- Controller code split into ControllerManager (seats), ControllerInputReader and MenuInput

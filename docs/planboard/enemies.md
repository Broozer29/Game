# Enemies
Regular enemies: their types, behaviour and how they spawn.

## Features
- Enemies and bosses scale with the player's power
- A screen that lists enemies, mini bosses and bosses, each shown once the player has met it
  - Bosses appear once defeated; mini bosses and regular enemies once encountered (Nelis, 2026-10-19)
